Unordered List in HTML

There are basically four types of unordered list in HTML
-None
-Disc
-Square
-Hollow circle

Disc(default)

  • Gargoyle
  • Labyrinth
  • Pendulum
  • Obsidian
  • Nebula

Circle(hollow circle)

  • Gargoyle
  • Labyrinth
  • Pendulum
  • Obsidian
  • Nebula

Square

  • Gargoyle
  • Labyrinth
  • Pendulum
  • Obsidian
  • Nebula

-None

  • Gargoyle
  • Labyrinth
  • Pendulum
  • Obsidian
  • Nebula
